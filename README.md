<p>
I'm a Data Analyst based in Nairobi, Kenya, with over 10 years of experience in data analytics, monitoring and evaluation, public health data, and humanitarian programmes. I currently serve as a Data Analytics &amp; Impact Officer at World Relief, supporting multi-country programmes through data systems strengthening, impact measurement, data quality assurance, and evidence-informed decision-making. Previously, I worked with NASCOP and KEMRI, supporting health information management and data analytics.
</p>

<p>
My work focuses on transforming complex field data, often collected through ODK and KoboToolbox, into actionable insights using Power BI, R, and advanced data visualization. I enjoy building analytical solutions, developing interactive dashboards, strengthening data systems, and creating clear visual stories that help programme teams understand performance and make informed decisions.
</p>
